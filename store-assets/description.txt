Marketplace Map enhances your Facebook Marketplace experience by displaying listings on an interactive map.

Browse by city or neighborhood, compare prices and photos at a glance, and quickly explore nearby offers — every listing you scroll past becomes a pin on the map.

FEATURES
• Interactive map — a pin for every listing; hover for a photo-and-price preview, click to open the listing
• Smart filters — set a min–max price range, show free items only, or keep just your saved favorites on the map
• Bookmarks — star interesting listings and spot them instantly as gold pins
• Color-coded pins — highlight free items and fresh "just listed" finds at a glance
• Auto-load — optionally scrolls the page once on load to pull more listings onto the map
• Draggable, minimizable window that stays out of your way while you browse

FAST & PRIVACY-FRIENDLY
Uses smart offline location matching to place listings accurately while keeping everything fast and privacy-friendly. No account, no setup, and your browsing data never leaves your device.

Perfect for buyers who want a clear geographic overview of available Marketplace listings.
